This notice covers the Praxmodoro website ({{SITE_HOST}}) and its beta waitlist. Praxmodoro is made by Prax, an independent developer based in India, who decides how this information is used (the “data fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). You can reach Prax at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • We collect your email address and anything you choose to tell us in the form.
  • We use it only to contact you about the Praxmodoro beta.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and we never sell or share your data.
  • Ask us at any time and we delete it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and the time you ticked the box agreeing to beta emails.
  • Optional answers, only if you give them: which kind of Mac you use, and what you find hardest to start (up to 280 characters).
  • Where the sign-up came from: which form on the page you used, campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source), and the address of the website that linked you here, if your browser shares it.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address. Your IP address is combined with a secret value and scrambled with SHA-256 before it is stored. We never store the address itself, and the hash can’t practically be turned back into it. We use it to limit how often one connection can submit the form.

We don’t ask for your name, and you don’t need an account. The Praxmodoro app itself keeps your sessions on your Mac; this notice is only about the website and waitlist.

Why we use it

  • To email you about the beta: invitations, important changes, and when it ends. This is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To plan testing, using your optional answers.
  • To keep the form from being abused, using the hashed IP address.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database, and the website runs on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare acts as our hosting provider and may process data in data centres outside India. Only Prax can download the list.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up until the beta ends, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. The short-term counters used for rate limiting are deleted automatically within 24 hours.

Your choices and rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for a summary of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw your consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it: use the unsubscribe link in any beta email, or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed up with. We aim to reply within a week.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Use cookies or tracking pixels on this website.
  • Ask about health conditions or diagnoses. Praxmodoro is not a medical service.

Age

The waitlist is for people aged 18 or over.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we’ll update the date at the top and, if you are on the waitlist, tell you by email.
