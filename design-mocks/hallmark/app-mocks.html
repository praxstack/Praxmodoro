Surface policy

Keep the promise visible, not the whole system.

Every compact surface has three jobs: show the one task, expose the next action, and offer an escape hatch for a thought or break.

  • Keyboard-complete; no hover-only commands.
  • Canonical timestamps survive sleep and restart.
  • Reduced Transparency uses solid semantic surfaces.
  • The menu bar can be the only visible surface.
The timer counts minutes. The product protects the return. Hallmark design exploration · research-backed, non-medical positioning
