Direction gate 2 · candidates

Three convictions, not three moods.

The first gate offered three directions that shared a temperament — all of them calm, soft and gentle — and the choice did not survive contact. These three disagree with each other about what a focus tool should feel like.

Nothing here is approved. These are candidates for issue #5. The previously approved direction (Living Companion) is linked at the bottom for comparison and remains the binding reference until a decision is recorded. Each candidate re-voices the product's five headlines in its own temperament — the copy is part of the direction, not a placeholder.

D · Flight Instrument

precisiondark groundtabularsub-200ms

For the person who wants the tool to take their work as seriously as they do. Time reads like an altimeter: dense numerals, hairline rules, nothing decorative. Motion is mechanical and exact — the instrument responds, it does not emote.

E · Quiet Terminal

monospacenear-monochrometext-as-interfacealmost no motion

For the person who experiences decoration as noise. Everything is text; the check-in is a prompt line. One hot accent does all the signalling. State changes are typographic rather than animated — the screen simply says something different.

F · Broadsheet

serif displayhard gridink on paperpage-like

For the person who wants beauty with spine rather than softness. Huge serif display type, a hard grid, generous white — the timer is the typography. Motion behaves like turning a page: deliberate, weighty, never fussy.
