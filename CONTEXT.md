# Praxodoro

Praxodoro is a native macOS focus timer and non-clinical ADHD-aware focus coach. This glossary fixes the language of the focus loop: how a stretch of work is opened, divided, interrupted, recovered from, and looked back on.

## Language

**Session**:
One continuous engagement with a single Task, opened by declaring what the work is and how it will be timed. Contains one or more Blocks and the Breaks between them.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, sitting, run

**Block**:
One continuous run of focused work inside a Session, between Breaks. A Block traverses the Phases the Session's timing policy prescribes. Check-ins occur within a Block.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, interval, cycle, round, sprint

**Phase**:
One segment of a Block, prescribed by the Session's timing policy — an entry ramp, a focus stretch, an open-ended flow stretch, or a recovery ramp. Timed or open-ended.
_Avoid_: stage, segment, period

**Break**:
A deliberate recovery interval between two Blocks of the same Session, carrying the means to re-enter the Task.
_Avoid_: rest, intermission, recess

**Task**:
The single piece of work a Session is devoted to. A Session never changes its Task.
_Avoid_: to-do, item, project, goal

**Step**:
The smallest concrete move currently in front of the person, small enough to begin without deciding anything. Re-declared as often as needed within a Session.
_Avoid_: first action, next action, move, sub-task

**Detour**:
Attention moved to something outside the Task. Recovery means returning to the Task.
_Avoid_: distraction, interruption

**Day**:
The Sessions that occurred within one calendar day. A way of looking at Sessions, not something a person starts or finishes.
_Avoid_: workday, timeline
