## Top-Level Questions

- Which competitor capabilities create real user value, and which create friction or subscription resentment?
- What ADHD-related needs can a focus coach support without making medical claims or increasing cognitive load?
- Which timer, check-in, task-initiation, adaptive-break, accountability, reflection, blocking, automation, analytics, and integration behaviors belong in the product?
- What advanced Liquid Glass and modern skeuomorphic visual language can feel distinctive, legible, accessible, and native to macOS?
- Which Apple frameworks and open-source libraries can implement those effects and behaviors reliably?
- What should ship first, what should be staged, and what should be rejected?

## Notes

- Inclusion boundary: native macOS focus/Pomodoro/time-tracking products with meaningful timer, task, blocking, analytics, automation, or cross-device features; selected adjacent ADHD and automatic-time-tracking products; Apple-native and well-maintained open-source UI/effects libraries.
- Exclusions: cloning proprietary branding or artwork, medical diagnosis/treatment claims, broad generic task managers with no focus workflow, and unmaintained visual-effect demos without a plausible production path.
- Capability profile: live web search and page inspection available; local files, code execution, screenshots, image generation, and writable artifacts available; PDF/vision available; no delegated subagents used; verification is a fresh sequential falsification pass rather than model-independent.
