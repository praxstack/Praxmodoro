# Research Plan

## Mission

Build an evidence-backed research package for Praxodoro ADHD-aware macOS focus coach, premium Pomodoro landscape, liquid-glass skeuomorphic UX, and implementation feasibility.

## Questions

- What evidence-backed product, interaction, visual, accessibility, and technical design should define a best-in-class native macOS ADHD-aware focus coach?

## Shard Strategy

- Determine shard axes after the scout wave.

## Source Priorities

- Primary sources first
- Recent sources for unstable facts
- Community sources for practitioner signal

## Wave Strategy

- Start with a scout wave
- Default maximum waves: 5

## Stop Conditions

- Stop after two consecutive low-yield waves or when major questions have strong evidence.
