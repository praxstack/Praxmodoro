# Wave 2 Plan

## Objective

Adversarially verify wave 1 by trying to disprove the adaptive-coach thesis, bound claims that are not directly supported, and turn platform or dependency risk into staged product decisions.

## Verification Shards

1. `w2-breaks-adhd-falsification`: compare contradictory break studies, separate ADHD-specific evidence from general productivity evidence, and bound body-doubling or AI-coach claims.
2. `w2-platform-feasibility`: verify CloudKit timing semantics, app and website blocking requirements, EventKit permissions, App Intents support, and on-device Foundation Models availability.
3. `w2-effects-dependency-audit`: inspect candidate repositories locally for licensing, release recency, transitive dependencies, platform targets, tests, and accessibility behavior.
4. `w2-product-scope-adjudication`: challenge the proposed feature set against activation cost, privacy, platform risk, and differentiation; classify ship-first, staged, experimental, and rejected capabilities.

## Falsification Questions

- Does evidence justify automatic adaptive break prescriptions, or only user-steerable options?
- Is a digital accountability or body-double feature proven enough for efficacy claims?
- Can automatic CloudKit mirroring provide a live synchronized countdown?
- Can reliable website blocking ship as an ordinary sandboxed feature without privileged components?
- Do third-party effects materially improve the core visual system enough to justify their maintenance and accessibility burden?
- Does the feature-rich thesis collapse under the low-cognitive-load requirement?

## Success Condition

Downgrade unsupported claims, preserve explicit uncertainty, and produce a defensible product/technical scope whose core value survives removal of AI, blocking, cross-device real-time control, and third-party visual dependencies.
